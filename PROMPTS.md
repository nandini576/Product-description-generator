# Week 7 - AI Prompt Log

## System Prompt

You are an expert food marketing copywriter. Generate engaging, professional, and SEO-friendly product descriptions that highlight freshness, nutrition, and the value of products sourced from regional farmers.

---

## Prompt Version 1

### Prompt

Generate a product description.

Product Name: Organic Foxtail Millet

Category: Millets

Tone: Professional

Features:
- Rich in Fiber
- Gluten Free
- Naturally Grown
- No Preservatives

### Example Output

Organic Foxtail Millet is a premium-quality grain naturally grown without harmful chemicals. Rich in fiber and gluten-free, it is an excellent choice for healthy meals. Carefully processed to preserve its nutrients, it offers both taste and nutrition.

## Prompt Version 2

### Prompt

Generate a marketing product description for the following food product.

Product Name: Organic Foxtail Millet

Category: Millets

Tone: Marketing

Features:
- Rich in Fiber
- Gluten Free
- Naturally Grown
- No Preservatives
- Sourced from Regional Farmers

Highlight:
- Health benefits
- Freshness
- Support for local farmers

Experience the goodness of Organic Foxtail Millet, carefully cultivated by regional farmers using natural farming practices. Rich in fiber and naturally gluten-free, this wholesome grain supports a healthy lifestyle while delivering authentic taste. Free from preservatives and packed with essential nutrients, it is perfect for nutritious everyday meals.

## Prompt Version 3

### Prompt

Write an SEO-friendly e-commerce product description.

Product Name: Organic Foxtail Millet

Category: Millets

Tone: Luxury

Features:
- Organic
- Premium Quality
- Farm Fresh
- Rich in Fiber

Keep the description under 120 words.

### Example Output

Organic Foxtail Millet is a premium farm-fresh grain known for its exceptional quality and nutritional value. Naturally rich in fiber and grown using sustainable farming methods, it is an ideal addition to a healthy diet. Carefully sourced and processed, it delivers freshness, taste, and wellness in every serving.

---

# Best Prompt

Prompt Version 2 produced the best results because it generated a more natural and engaging description. It highlighted the health benefits, freshness, and contribution of regional farmers, making the content more suitable for an e-commerce platform. The response was informative, well-structured, and aligned with the project's objective of promoting food products.
